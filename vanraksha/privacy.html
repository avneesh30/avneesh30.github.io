Vanraksha: The Spirit of the Forest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Vanraksha (package com.vanraksha.game) is a game made by Avneesh Kumar ("we"). This page explains what data the game uses.

Data the game itself keeps

The game saves your progress (episodes finished, current area, coins, settings such as language and touch controls) only on your device. We do not have accounts, we do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and we do not send your game progress to any server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free game shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the main menu, an occasional full-screen ad between areas, and an optional "watch an ad to refill hearts" ad in the pause menu. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version) and ad interactions (views, taps),
  • crash and performance diagnostics of the ads SDK.

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and sharing

We do not sell personal data. Apart from the ads SDK described above, the game does not share data with third parties. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data removes all game data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
